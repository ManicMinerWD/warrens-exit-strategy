Carried-forward rental losses — the loss offset negative gearing

Since purchase in 2007 and continuing through the rental period, the 5/143 Sussex Street investment has been negatively geared for part of each year — rental income has sometimes been less than the holding costs (interest + body corp + rates + property management + maintenance), producing a tax loss. These losses are carried forward and, as a long-term rental, can be used to offset the capital gain on the eventual sale of the property, eliminating the CGT.
