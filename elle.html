ELLE Resort & Beach Club — Batu Belig, Bali

Option 1 — 168-key branded resort + landmark beach club, Seminyak. Individual suite sale into a 50-year managed investment program. Geonet / SONO / Inspiral, Stage 1, June 2026.

Last updated:
Read this first. Every rate, occupancy and return figure below is a Geonet / SONO / Inspiral projection, not a contract and not independently validated. Concept design — artist impressions and indicative areas, subject to change, approval and final documentation.

A 5-star beachfront development of 168 branded suites and a landmark beach club on two consolidated sites in Batu Belig, between Seminyak and Canggu. Developed by Geonet Developments International, operated by SONO Hotels & Resorts Asia, designed by Inspiral, project-managed by Colliers. Suites are sold individually into a 50-year investment period inside a fully managed rental program, with an 8% guaranteed capital repayment during construction and up to 15% p.a. projected net returns off a 45% revenue pool spanning rooms, F&B and beach club operations.

The asset — two sites, two collections Inspiral Stage 1 · 52-spread brochure

The development holds two separate accommodation offers on two consolidated sites split by a small road. The Resort sits on Site 1 with 130 keys across thirteen types. The Beach Club Suites sit on Site 2 with 38 keys across eight types, joined to the beach club itself by a tunnel and a sky bridge.

The Beach Club Suites — 38 keys, 8 types

Why the areas are quoted this way. The convention checks out by arithmetic: internal plus counted outdoor reproduces the brochure's own stated totals of 7,994 m² for the Resort and 2,702 m² for the Suites, exactly. It holds in every type except One Bedroom Suite B, where the source states a 5 m² balcony with 3 m² counted.
Two inconsistencies worth knowing. The per-level unit summaries printed on the drawings do not fully reconcile to the masterplan schedules — on the Resort, standard rooms tally 86 against 94 and split-level lofts and penthouses appear on each level they occupy; on the Suites, the level drawings account for 37 suites against 38 (one Regular Suite C short). The masterplan schedules are treated as the figure of record. Separately, the Stage 1 brochure holds building-level floor plans only — there is no per-type unit plan for any room or suite, which would have to be requested from Inspiral.

Part one — the market Bali 2025 actuals · Horwath HTL 2026

The luxury tier is the only one growing.

  • Bali took a record 6.95 million international arrivals in 2025. 2026 target 6.63m; 2028–2030 projection 8–9m annually.
  • A 146% surge in private villa supply over two years has softened the broader hotel market — but the pressure sits in the mid-market and economy tiers. Luxury and upper upscale held.
  • 2025 ADR growth: economy +2.4% (A$235) · upper upscale +10% (A$446–455) · luxury +8.0% (A$1,280, tier above A$785).
  • Australia is 25% of arrivals — the source market. IDR/USD 16,000; luxury growth +8.0% in IDR, +3.9% in USD.
ELLE's stated positioning band: A$469–782 — it sits in the luxury tier that is holding, not the tiers absorbing the new villa supply.

Part four — the ceiling moves Market acceptance

Where guests start choosing Regent. The market begins to reject ELLE pricing at the point where a guest can choose Regent Bali Canggu at a comparable or lower rate for a demonstrably superior product. That ceiling is not permanent — as the identity crystallises through events, editorial and social proof, it rises. Where this lands Against Warren's criteria

Caveats & what to demand before committing

  • Nothing on this page is a guarantee. The 15% p.a., the 8% construction repayment, the ADR bands and the occupancy targets are all projections from a marketing document dated June 2026, premised on a 2028 opening that has not happened yet.
  • Define the 45% pool precisely. Net of what — operating costs, SONO management fee, debt service, reserves? A pool that is "45% net" of an undefined base can be made to produce almost any headline number.
  • Establish who stands behind the 8%. A construction-period capital repayment guarantee is only worth the balance sheet of the guarantor. Ask for the instrument, not the sentence.
  • Get the exit terms in writing. What happens at year 50? What happens if the operator defaults, if the pool underperforms, or if you want out at year 7? Resale in a secondary market for an off-plan branded suite is not a liquid asset.
  • Test the beach club assumption independently. The whole premium rests on the beach club performing. "800 day-pass admissions per week" and "40% of beach club revenue from non-hotel guests" are the two numbers to pressure. Which comparable Seminyak beach clubs actually draw 800 day-passes in a peak week?
  • There is no operating track record to anchor the pool — unlike FINNS, where the beach club already runs and the 256-room resort is being added to a known operation. SONO is a capable regional operator, but 16 hotels across two countries is not a track record at this scale in Bali.
  • Compare against the alternatives on the same basis. Casa Petak is a completed 3BR villa you own outright, with a named manager and a 20% fee you can read. FINNS is a 35% gross-revenue share of an operating club. ELLE is cheaper to enter and higher on paper — but it is also the least verifiable of the three.
