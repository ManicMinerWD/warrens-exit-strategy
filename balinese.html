Bali Income Strategy — Elle Beach Club $256K (personal property)

Personal property (no PT PMA, no Indonesia bonds, no IDX shares) · 10% Indonesia rental withholding · convert net IDR to SGD · deploy into personal portfolio

Last updated:

Strategy overview the flow: personal property (Elle) → FX conversion (net IDR to SGD) → personal portfolio (AI/robotics / sovereign AI shares)

You own the Elle Beach Club $256K investment directly as personal property — no PT PMA, no Indonesian corporate structure, no Indonesia bonds, no IDX shares. IDR rental income is collected against the asset; a 10% final withholding tax is applied to gross rentals; the net IDR is converted to SGD and deployed into your personal investment portfolio (AI/robotics / sovereign AI shares). The 10% Indonesian rental withholding is the only intentional leakage on the rental itself; the FX spread on the IDR-to-SGD conversion is the second. The villa's 10%+ net rental yield is the income engine that funds the personal portfolio.

The chain (3 stages)

  1. Personal ownership (you hold the $256K) — Elle Beach Club is owned directly by you as personal property. No PT PMA, no Indonesian corporate structure, no Indonesia bonds, no IDX shares. The rental yield is the income engine that funds everything downstream.
  2. Collect IDR rental — 10% Indonesia final withholding — rental income is received in IDR against the asset. Indonesia applies a 10% final withholding tax on gross rental income (the only tax on the rental itself). The net IDR is retained onshore.
  3. Convert net IDR to SGD — deploy into personal portfolio — net IDR (after the 10% rental withholding) is converted to SGD and deployed into your personal portfolio — AI/robotics / sovereign AI shares. No PT PMA, no Indonesia bonds, no IDX shares, no outbound dividend.

Note: because the Elle Beach Club is held as personal property (no PT PMA, no Indonesia bonds, no IDX shares), the PT PMA structure, the 6.5% SBN coupon regime, and the IDX domestic-share regime no longer apply to this strategy.
