Strategy overview the flow: villa → PT PMA → IDR → Elle → Finns → Indonesia dividends

The Bali income strategy is a single, coherent funnel: a completed STR villa (Elle Beach Club, $256K) and its operating partner (Finns, $100K) generate IDR rental income; after 10% final withholding tax, the after-tax IDR is retained in-country. Elle Beach Club ($256K) provides $25k/year income during the hotel build and into operation. Finns ($100K) is the operating partner: it requires 18 milestone payments while the Finns Beach Hotel is built, and Finns Beach Hotel also gives $25k/yr income once built. The $10k/month (18 monthly phases) is redeployed into debt reduction / capital build; the residual $25k/year from each asset is invested in Indonesian government bonds (6.5% coupon) in the PT PMA's name — all onshore, all in IDR, no FX conversion, no outbound dividend, no foreign capital gains tax. Step 1 — Register the PT PMA Indonesian foreign-owned LLC

A PT PMA (Penanaman Modal Asing) is Indonesia's vehicle for foreign direct investment. It is required for a foreigner to legally hold title/leasehold to Bali investment property, operate a rental business, and open a corporate bank account and bond positions. The PT PMA is owned by the foreign shareholder and managed by a local director or nominee manager (subject to Indonesian corporate law).

What a PT PMA gives you

CapabilityWhy you need it
Hold leasehold / HGB titleIndonesian law requires a PT PMA for foreign-owned property investment over a threshold. The villa leasehold is held in the PT PMA's name, not yours personally.
Sign management agreementThe operator (Finns, etc.) signs with the PT PMA — the entity that legally operates the rental. Your personal involvement is limited.
Receive IDR rental incomeRent goes to the PT PMA's bank account. The PT PMA is the taxable entity in Indonesia.
Open corporate bank accountMandiri (or BCA) corporate account in PT PMA name. Required to receive rental income and pay expenses.
Hold bond positions6.5% IDR SBN held in the PT PMA's name, onshore, in IDR, with no FX conversion.
File Indonesian corporate taxThe PT PMA files its own Indonesian corporate tax return. Rental income is taxed at the final 10% rate.
Do not skip the PT PMA. The villa cannot be held personally by a foreigner under Indonesian law (with limited exceptions). The PT PMA is the legally recognised vehicle. Trying to hold it informally exposes the structure to enforcement risk and can void the lease.

6.5% Indonesia bonds (SBN) — the income engine IDR · onshore · 6.5% coupon · no FX

The residual Bali rental income ($25k/year, equivalent to Rp 125,000,000/yr) is invested in Indonesian government bonds (SBN — Surat Berharga Negara) at a 6.5% coupon, held in the PT PMA's name, onshore, in IDR. The bonds are the primary income stream for this strategy: they produce a predictable annual coupon with no FX conversion, no outbound dividend, and no foreign capital gains tax. The coupon is the long-horizon income that supplements the villa rental.

The dividend route is the trap. A 10% rental tax is unavoidable in Indonesia. Adding a 20% outbound dividend withholding on the same money leaks ~30% of gross before it even reaches the investment account. Deployment as capital — rent → 18×$10k/m → $25k/yr → 6.5% IDR bonds — keeps the leakage at 10% + coupon withholding, with 0% FX, 0% outbound dividend, and 0% foreign capital gains. The new strategy is the coherent single-funnel approach.

Risks & things to confirm before wiring

1. PT PMA structure. Minimum capital threshold, nominee director arrangement, KBLI code, local office requirement, and NPWP/NIB issuance all need confirmation with independent Indonesian counsel.
2. Elle Beach Club $256K / Finns $100K. The revenue-share mechanics, the $100K equity stake, and the $256K investor pool terms must be confirmed with Finns and the resort operator — the 10% rental yield depends on the operated rate and the management agreement.
3. 18×$10k/m cash-flow split. The split between the $10k/month debt-reduction deployment and the $25k/yr bond allocation must be confirmed with the operator (Finns) — the model's split is a working assumption; the actual cash flow depends on the management agreement and the resort's operating statements.
4. 6.5% SBN coupon & coupon withholding. The 6.5% coupon rate and the coupon-interest withholding rate must be confirmed with Indonesian counsel and the Mandiri relationship manager. The coupon is the income engine; confirm the rate and the withholding treatment.
5. Villa exit CGT. The villa's exit capital gains tax must be confirmed with Indonesian counsel — the earlier Singapore 22% capital gains tax does not apply to the onshore IDR / SBN structure, but the 10% rental withholding and any Indonesian on-sale tax must be confirmed.
6. FX & outbound dividend — not applicable to the onshore deployment. Because the residual $25k/yr is invested in 6.5% IDR SBN held at the PT PMA, with no FX conversion and no outbound dividend, there is no FX risk and no outbound dividend withholding on the bond deployment.

  • Invest the residual in 6.5% Indonesia bonds (SBN) at the PT PMA (confirm the 6.5% coupon rate and the coupon-interest withholding rate with the Mandiri relationship manager) — the income engine.
  • Confirm villa exit CGT with Indonesian counsel (the earlier Singapore 22% capital gains tax does not apply to the onshore IDR / SBN structure).
